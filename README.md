# Adventure Works - End-to-End Data Analytics Project

## Project Overview
This project involves a comprehensive data analysis of the Adventure Works dataset. The objective was to extract actionable business insights and develop interactive dashboards and reports across four major technical modules: **Microsoft Excel, MySQL, Tableau, and Power BI**. 

**Author:** Aryan Maher  

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) Analyzed
The analysis focused on several core business metrics to evaluate performance:
* **Overall Metrics:** Achieved Total Sales of approximately $16.4M, generating a Total Profit of $6.79M against a Total Production Cost of $9.6M. 
* **Order Volume:** Processed a total of 54,771 orders.
* **Yearly Trends:** Analyzed sales across 2013 and 2014 to track annual growth.
* **Quarterly & Monthly Performance:** Monitored seasonal trends, showing consistent sales tracking across Q1 to Q4 and breaking down revenue month-by-month to identify peak periods like December, which reached over $1.87M in sales.
* **Cost Efficiency:** Compared Sum of Sales Amount against Total Product Cost per quarter to analyze profit margins over time.

## Tech Stack
* **Database Management:** MySQL (for querying and structuring relational data)
* **Spreadsheet Analysis:** Microsoft Excel (for initial KPI generation, pivot tables, and static dashboards)
* **Data Visualization:** Tableau & Power BI (for building dynamic, interactive dashboards)
